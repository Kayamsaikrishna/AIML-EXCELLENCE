03. Advanced Integration Techniques

Trig substitution and Partial Fractions.

1. Trigonometric Substitution

Used to eliminate square roots like \(\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}\) by letting \(x = a \sin \theta\). The Pythagorean identity \(\cos^2 \theta = 1 - \sin^2 \theta\) collapses the radical.

$$ \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} \to a \cos \theta $$

2. Partial Fraction Decomposition (PFD)

Splitting a complex rational function into simpler fractions that are easy to integrate (usually resulting in \(\ln\) functions):

$$ \frac{1}{(x-1)(x+2)} = \frac{A}{x-1} + \frac{B}{x+2} $$

3. AI Connection: Control Theory & Stability

Laplace Transforms

In Robotics, we often work in the frequency domain using Laplace Transforms. Integrating these transforms often requires Partial Fractions to decompose the system's transfer function. This allows the AI to determine if a robot's movement will be smooth or oscillate uncontrollably based on the "poles" of the fractions.

4. Python: Automated Decomposition

import sympy as sp

x = sp.Symbol('x')
f = 1 / (x**2 + 3*x + 2)

# Partial Fraction Decomposition
pfd = sp.apart(f)
print(f"Decomposition: {pfd}")

# Integrate
res = sp.integrate(f, x)
print(f"Integral: {res}") # results in logs
