03. Finding Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors

A step-by-step mechanical guide.

Step 1: The Eigenvalues

Solve \(\det(\mathbf{A} - \lambda\mathbf{I}) = 0\).

Numerical Example (2x2)

Let \(\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}\).

$$ \begin{vmatrix} 4-\lambda & 1 \\ 2 & 3-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = 0 $$ $$ (4-\lambda)(3-\lambda) - 2 = 12 - 7\lambda + \lambda^2 - 2 = 0 $$ $$ \lambda^2 - 7\lambda + 10 = 0 \implies (\lambda-5)(\lambda-2) = 0 $$

Results: \(\lambda_1 = 5, \lambda_2 = 2\).

Step 2: The Eigenvectors

For each \(\lambda\), solve the system \((\mathbf{A} - \lambda\mathbf{I})\mathbf{v} = 0\).

For \(\lambda_1 = 5\):

$$ \begin{bmatrix} 4-5 & 1 \\ 2 & 3-5 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} $$ $$ \begin{bmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 2 & -2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} $$

Both equations imply \(-x + y = 0 \implies x = y\). An eigenvector is \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\).

3. Python: Step-by-Step with Sympy

import sympy as sp

A = sp.Matrix([[4, 1], [2, 3]])

# Step 1: Eigenvalues
eigenvals = A.eigenvals()
print(f"Eigenvalues: {eigenvals}") # Returns value:multiplicity

# Step 2: Eigenvectors
eigenvects = A.eigenvects()
for val, mult, vects in eigenvects:
    print(f"Lambda = {val}: Vector = {vects[0]}")
