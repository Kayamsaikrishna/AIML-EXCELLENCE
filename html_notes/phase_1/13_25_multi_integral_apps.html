05. Applications: Mass and Center of Mass

Finding the balance point of a 2D distribution.

1. Total Mass

If \(\rho(x, y)\) is the density of a plate (\(\text{mass/area}\)), then the Total Mass \(M\) is the double integral of density over the region \(D\):

$$ M = \iint_{D} \rho(x, y) dA $$

2. The Center of Mass (Centroid)

The "average" location of the mass, \((\bar{x}, \bar{y})\), is found by dividing the Moments by the Total Mass:

$$ \bar{x} = \frac{1}{M} \iint_{D} x \rho(x, y) dA, \quad \bar{y} = \frac{1}{M} \iint_{D} y \rho(x, y) dA $$

This is the 2D versions of the expected value \(\mathbb{E}[X]\) and \(\mathbb{E}[Y]\).

3. AI/ML Connection: K-Means & Cluster Centroids

Gravity in the Data Space

In Unsupervised Learning, we often use the K-Means Clustering algorithm. The core of this algorithm is finding the "Centroid" of a group of data points. This centroid is exactly the Center of Mass if we assume each data point has unit mass.

Furthermore, and most importantly, in Image Processing, the "Centroid" of a detected object helps an AI track the movement of that object (e.g., following a person's head in a video). By integrating the pixel intensities, the AI can find the "True Center" even if the object is blurry or has an irregular shape. This is the foundation of Object Tracking and Motion Estimation.

4. Python: Centroid of a Region

# Find centroid of a unity-density square [0,1]x[0,1]
# Mass = 1. Moment_x = int(x dx dy) = 0.5. x_bar = 0.5/1 = 0.5.

from scipy.integrate import dblquad

mass, _ = dblquad(lambda y, x: 1, 0, 1, 0, 1)
mom_x, _ = dblquad(lambda y, x: x, 0, 1, 0, 1)

x_bar = mom_x / mass

print(f"Center of Mass coord (x): {x_bar}")
