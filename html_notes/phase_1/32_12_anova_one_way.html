12. Analysis of Variance (ANOVA): One-way

Comparing multiple groups without the "t-test trap."

1. Why ANOVA?

If you have three groups (A, B, C) and you want to know if they are different, why not just run three t-tests (A-B, B-C, A-C)? Because of the Multiple Comparison Problem. Each test has a 5% chance of a Type I error. With 3 tests, your total risk of a false positive jumps significantly. ANOVA solves this by running a single "Omnibus" test to see if *any* group is different from the others.

2. The F-Statistic: Variance Ratios

ANOVA calculates an **F-score**, which is the ratio of:

$$ F = \frac{\text{Variance BETWEEN groups}}{\text{Variance WITHIN groups}} $$

If $F$ is large, it means the groups are moved far apart from each other relative to how much noise is inside them. This implies a significant difference between at least two groups.

3. AI/ML Connection: Multi-Model Benchmarking

The "Leaderboard" Paradox

In AI, ANOVA is the standard for **Tournament Evaluation**.

Imagine you are comparing four different AI models for **Self-Driving perception**: Model A (CNN), Model B (Vision Transformer), Model C (Hybrid), and Model D (Legacy). You test them on 30 different weather scenarios. You have 4 groups of data. If you only look at the averages, you might crown Model C as the winner. But an **ANOVA** will reveal if the differences are statistically significant or if the leaderboard positions are just random noise. In **Reinforcement Learning**, where training is notoriously unstable, ANOVA is used to compare different **Policy Gradient algorithms** across multiple seeds. It tells the researcher: "Don't bother with these fancy tweaks; none of them are statistically better than the base algorithm yet."

4. Python: f_oneway

# Model A, B, and C scores across 10 trials
model_a = [0.81, 0.83, 0.80, 0.82]
model_b = [0.85, 0.86, 0.84, 0.85]
model_c = [0.82, 0.82, 0.81, 0.83]

f_stat, p_val = stats.f_oneway(model_a, model_b, model_c)

print(f"F-statistic: {f_stat:.2f}, p-value: {pval:.4f}")
# If p < 0.05, at least one model is significantly different from the rest. 
# (Usually model B is the standout here).
