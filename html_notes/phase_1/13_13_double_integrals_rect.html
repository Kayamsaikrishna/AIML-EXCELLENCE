Double Integrals

Volume under the surface.

From Area to Volume

Single integrals \( \int f(x) dx \) calculate Area under a curve. Double integrals \( \iint f(x, y) dA \) calculate Volume under a surface \( z = f(x, y) \) over a specific region \( R \).

If the region \( R \) is a rectangle \([a, b] \times [c, d]\), the calculation is straightforward: \[ \iint_R f(x,y) dA = \int_a^b \int_c^d f(x,y) \, dy \, dx \] We perform "partial integration" (integrate w.r.t one variable while holding the other constant) twice.

2. AI/ML Connection: Joint Probability

Probability Mass

In Statistics and ML, if \( X \) and \( Y \) are continuous random variables with a joint probability density function (PDF) \( p(x, y) \), then the probability that a data point falls into a region \( R \) is: \[ P((X, Y) \in R) = \iint_R p(x, y) \, dx \, dy \]

This is fundamental for Classification. To classify a point, we often integrate likelihood densities over specific regions of the feature space. If the integral evaluates to 1 over the entire space, it's a valid probability distribution.

3. Python: Numerical Integration

double_integral.py
from scipy.integrate import dblquad
import numpy as np

# Function: f(x, y) = x * y^2
def f(y, x):
    return x * y**2

# Integrate over x in [0, 2], y in [0, 1]
# Note: dblquad expects function args as (y, x)
area, error = dblquad(f, 0, 2, lambda x: 0, lambda x: 1)

print(f"Volume: {area:.4f}")
print(f"Error Estimate: {error:.4e}")

# Theoretical Result:
# Int(x) from 0 to 2 = [x^2/2] = 2
# Int(y^2) from 0 to 1 = [y^3/3] = 1/3
# Total = 2 * 1/3 = 0.6666...
