06. Area Between Curves and Volumes

Geometric applications of the definite integral.

1. Area Between Two Curves

If \(f(x) \geq g(x)\) on \([a, b]\), the area between them is:

$$ A = \int_a^b [f(x) - g(x)] dx $$

2. Volumes of Revolution

  • Disk Method: Rotating \(f(x)\) around x-axis: \( V = \int_a^b \pi [f(x)]^2 dx \).
  • Washer Method: Rotating gap between \(f\) and \(g\): \( V = \int_a^b \pi ([f(x)]^2 - [g(x)]^2) dx \).

3. AI Connection: Model Divergence

Earth Mover's Distance (EMD)

In Generative AI (GANs), we need to measure how different two probability distributions are. The area between the two "landscapes" (curves) represents the "effort" needed to transform one into the other. This "area between curves" logic is the geometric basis for the **Wasserstein Loss**, which prevents GANs from collapsing during training.

4. Python: Area Computation

import sympy as sp

x = sp.Symbol('x')
f = x**2
g = x

# 1. Find intersection points (0 and 1)
pts = sp.solve(f - g, x)

# 2. Integrate top - bottom on [0, 1]
area = sp.integrate(g - f, (x, 0, 1))

print(f"Area between y=x and y=x^2 on [0, 1]: {area}")
