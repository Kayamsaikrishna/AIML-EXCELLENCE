09. Recommender Systems & Matrix Factorization

Predicting user preferences through latent factors.

1. The Rating Matrix (\(\mathbf{R}\))

Imagine a matrix where rows are Users and columns are Movies. Most entries are empty (Sparse). Our goal is to fill the blanks.

$$ \mathbf{R} \approx \mathbf{U}\mathbf{V}^T $$
  • \(\mathbf{U}\): User Preferences (Latent traits).
  • \(\mathbf{V}\): Movie Characteristics (Latent traits).

2. Low-Rank Approximation

We force the decomposition to have a small inner dimension \(k\) (rank). This forces the model to find general patterns (e.g., "This user likes Action movies") rather than memorizing individual ratings.

3. AI Insight: SVD vs Alternating Least Squares

Classic SVD requires a full matrix (no zeros). In RecSys, we have missing data. We use ALS (Alternating Least Squares) or Stochastic Gradient Descent to optimize the fragments \(\mathbf{U}\) and \(\mathbf{V}\) to match the known entries of \(\mathbf{R}\).

4. Python: Predicting a Rating

import numpy as np

# Latent factor for User A (e.g., [likes_action, likes_comedy])
u_a = np.array([0.9, 0.1])

# Latent factor for Movie X (e.g., [is_action, is_comedy])
v_x = np.array([0.8, -0.2])

# Prediction is a Dot Product!
rating = np.dot(u_a, v_x)

print(f"Predicted Rating for User A on Movie X: {rating:.2f}")
