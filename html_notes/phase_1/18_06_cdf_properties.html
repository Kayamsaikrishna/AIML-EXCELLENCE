Prob 1.4 Inversion

CDF Properties

From probability to quantiles.

The Inverse CDF

Since the CDF \(F_X(x)\) is monotonic, it is invertible. The inverse function \(F^{-1}(p)\) tells us: "What value of \(x\) has \(p\) probability mass below it?" This is the Quantile Function.

2. ML Connection: Sampling

Inverse Transform Sampling

How do computers generate random numbers from a Gaussian or Exponential distribution? Usually, they generate a Uniform random number \(u \sim U[0, 1]\). Then they apply the inverse CDF: \(x = F^{-1}(u)\). This transforms the uniform noise into the desired distribution.
