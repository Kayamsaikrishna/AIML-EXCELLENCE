11. Related Rates and Differentials

Differentiation in multi-variable connected systems.

1. The Concept

If two variables \(x\) and \(y\) are related by an equation, and both change over time \(t\), their rates of change \(\frac{dx}{dt}\) and \(\frac{dy}{dt}\) are also related via the Chain Rule.

2. Linear Approximation (Differentials)

For a tiny change \(dx\), the change in \(y\) is approximately:

$$ dy \approx f'(x) \cdot dx $$

This is the foundation of Sensitivity Analysis.

3. AI Connection: Parameter Sensitivity

Vulnerability to Perturbations

In "Adversarial AI", we want to know how a tiny change in pixel values (\(dx\)) leads to a massive change in the prediction (\(dy\)). Related rates and differentials allow us to quantify the Jacobian of the network—the matrix that maps input perturbations to output shifts. If the "related rate" is high, the model is brittle/unstable.

4. Python: Related Rates Simulation

import sympy as sp

# Area of circle A = pi * r^2
# Find dA/dt if dr/dt = 2 and r = 5
t = sp.Symbol('t')
r = sp.Function('r')(t)
A = sp.pi * r**2

# Differentiate with respect to t
dAdt = sp.diff(A, t)
print(f"dA/dt formula: {dAdt}")

# plug in values
# dAdt = 2 * pi * r * dr/dt
result = 2 * sp.pi * 5 * 2
print(f"Result: {result} units^2/sec")
