08. The Quotient Rule

Differentiating ratios and fractions.

1. The Rule

If \(f(x) = \frac{u(x)}{v(x)}\), the derivative is calculated as:

$$ \frac{d}{dx} \left[ \frac{u}{v} \right] = \frac{v \frac{du}{dx} - u \frac{dv}{dx}}{v^2} $$

"Low D-High minus High D-Low, over the square of what's below."

2. Common Difficulty

The order of subtraction in the numerator is critical. Reversing \(vu'\) and \(uv'\) will result in the wrong sign. Always start with the bottom function (\(v\)).

3. AI/ML Connection: The Softmax Function

Gradient of Probabilities

In classification, the Softmax function creates probabilities by dividing the exponent of one class by the sum of exponents of all classes:

$$ \sigma(z)_i = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum e^{z_j}} $$

When computing how a small change in inputs affects these probabilities during training, we must use the Quotient Rule. This is why the gradient of Softmax has a specific "p(1-p)" or "p_i(delta_ij - p_j)" structure.

4. Python: Symbolic Quotient Differentiator

import sympy as sp

x = sp.Symbol('x')
u = sp.exp(x)
v = x + 1

# Calculating d/dx [e^x / (x+1)]
quotient_deriv = sp.simplify(sp.diff(u / v, x))

print(f"Derivative: {quotient_deriv}")
# Output: (x*exp(x))/(x + 1)**2
