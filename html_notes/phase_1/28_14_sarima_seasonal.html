Stats 1.8 Periodic patterns

SARIMA (Seasonal ARIMA)

Handling the cyclical heartbeat of data. How to model patterns that repeat every week, month, or year.

The Seasonal Extension

Pure ARIMA is great for trends, but it's blind to "Calendar Effects".

SARIMA adds a secondary set of parameters to model the relationship between observations separated by a full period (e.g., comparing this Monday to last Monday).

1. The Parameter Set

$$ \text{SARIMA}(p, d, q) \times (P, D, Q)_s $$

Seasonal AR (\(P\))

The relationship between today and the same time point in previous periods (e.g., this July vs. last July).

Seasonal I (\(D\))

Seasonal differencing (\(Y_t - Y_{t-s}\)) to remove periodic trends.

Seasonal MA (\(Q\))

Modeling errors from previous seasonal periods.

2. Common Seasonal Periods (\(s\))

  • Weekly Data: \(s=7\) (Patterns repeat every 7 days).
  • Monthly Data: \(s=120\) (Patterns repeat every year).
  • Quarterly Data: \(s=4\) (Financial cycles).
  • Hourly Data: \(s=24\) (Daily patterns of electricity or web traffic).

3. ML Connection: Modern Forecasting Frameworks

Product ML Engineering

Prophet vs. SARIMA

Meta (Facebook) released Prophet, which has become the industry favorite for seasonal forecasting.

  • Handling Holidays: SARIMA struggles with "Floating Holidays" (like Easter or Diwali) because they don't happen on a fixed frequency. Prophet models these explicitly as user-defined events.
  • Multiple Seasonalities: Modern web traffic often has *Daily* AND *Weekly* seasonality. SARIMA can only handle one. ML models like N-BEATS or TFT use complex stacking to handle multiple overlapping cycles.
  • Uncertainty: In SARIMA, uncertainty is often linear. In Bayesian models (like those in PyMC), we can model the "Seasonal Variance" as something that changes—allowing for "Wild" holiday seasons vs. "Stable" summer months.
Engineer's Pro-Tip: If you have missing data or outliers, SARIMA will break. Its recursive nature means one bad data point poisons the whole forecast. Use Interpolation or switch to a Robust ML model if your data stream is messy.
