34. Double & Half Angle Formulas

Scaling frequency and resolution.

1. Double Angle Formulas

$$ \sin(2\theta) = 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta $$ $$ \cos(2\theta) = \cos^2 \theta - \sin^2 \theta = 2\cos^2 \theta - 1 = 1 - 2\sin^2 \theta $$ $$ \tan(2\theta) = \frac{2 \tan \theta}{1 - \tan^2 \theta} $$

2. Half Angle Formulas

$$ \sin\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) = \pm \sqrt{\frac{1 - \cos \theta}{2}} \quad \cos\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) = \pm \sqrt{\frac{1 + \cos \theta}{2}} $$

2. Computer Vision Feature Engineering

Edge Detection & Texture Synthesis

In classical Computer Vision (like HOG features or SIFT), we often analyze gradients at different scales. Double/Half angle formulas allow us to efficiently compute correlations between gradients directed at different angles without expensive inverse trig calls.

  • Frequency Multiplication: Used in Implicit Neural Representations to generate fine-grained texture detail from coarse spatial coordinates.
  • Power Reduction: The formulas \( \sin^2 \theta = \frac{1-\cos 2\theta}{2} \) are used to linearize squared trig terms in physical simulations.

3. Python: Frequency Doubling

import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

x = np.linspace(0, 2*np.pi, 200)

# Comparing original vs Double Angle
y1 = np.sin(x)
y2 = 2 * np.sin(x) * np.cos(x) # sin(2x) using formula

plt.plot(x, y1, label='sin(x)')
plt.plot(x, y2, label='sin(2x) via Identity')
plt.legend()
plt.title("Doubling Frequency via Trig Identity")
plt.show()
