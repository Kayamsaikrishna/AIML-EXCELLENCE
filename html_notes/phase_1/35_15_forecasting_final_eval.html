15. Time Series Evaluation & Future Forecasting

Validating the future before it happens.

1. No Shuffling!

In standard ML, we shuffle data. In Time Series, Shuffling is a Crime**. You cannot use tomorrow to predict today. We use **Walk-forward Validation** (or Time-Series Cross-Validation) where we train on the first $p$ steps and test on step $p+1$.

2. Specific Metrics

  • MAE: Mean Absolute Error. Easy to understand ($ error).
  • MAPE: Mean Absolute Percentage Error. Great for business ("The model is off by 5%").
  • RMSE: Root Mean Square Error. Penalizes large "Surprise" errors more heavily.

3. AI/ML Connection: Backtesting and Automated Pipelines

Reality vs. Simulation

In AI, time series evaluation is the core of Backtesting Systems.

In Algorithmic Trading**, we "Simulate" the past 10 years to see how our AI would have performed. Walk-forward validation prevents **Look-ahead Bias**, where the AI accidentally sees a future price spike and "Guesses" correctly only in the simulation. This is also how we build Model Monitoring Dashboards**: if the MAPE increases from 5% to 15%, the AI system triggers a **Retraining Pipeline**. Understanding evaluation is what allows AI to move from "Theoretical Success" to "Production Stability." It is the final verification that your model is actually capturing the physics of time.

4. Python: Walk-forward Simulation

from sklearn.model_selection import TimeSeriesSplit

tscv = TimeSeriesSplit(n_splits=5)

for train_index, test_index in tscv.split(data):
    # Model learns on past, predicts next 'test' block
    model_fit(data[train_index])
    score = eval(data[test_index])
    
# Averaging the scores gives the true 'Ability' of the AI!
