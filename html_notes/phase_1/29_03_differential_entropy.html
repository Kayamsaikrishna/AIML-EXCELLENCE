Differential Entropy

Expanding the concept of uncertainty to the infinite world of continuous probability density functions (PDFs).

The Continuous Paradox

In discrete variables, entropy measures the "Average bits of information." But in continuous space, an exact value has zero probability. To solve this, we define Differential Entropy, which measures the uncertainty of the *shape* of the distribution.

1. Definition

For a continuous random variable \(X\) with probability density function \(f(x)\), the differential entropy \(h(X)\) is:

$$ h(X) = - \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x) \log f(x) \, dx $$
Crucial Warning: Unlike Shannon Entropy (discrete), Differential Entropy can be negative!

Example: A uniform distribution on \([0, 0.5]\) has \(h(X) = \log(0.5) = -1\) bit. A negative value simply means the distribution is more "concentrated" than the base unit of the log.

2. Common Distributions

1. Uniform Distribution

For \(X \sim U(a, b)\):

$$ h(X) = \log(b-a) $$

Wide intervals = High entropy. Narrow intervals = Low entropy.

2. Normal Distribution

For \(X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)\):

$$ h(X) = \frac{1}{2} \log(2\pi e \sigma^2) $$

Only the variance (\(\sigma^2\)) affects entropy. The location (\(\mu\)) doesn't change how uncertain the distribution is.

3. The Maximum Entropy Principle

One of the most profound results in information theory:

**Among all continuous distributions with a given variance \(\sigma^2\), the Normal (Gaussian) distribution has the MAXIMUM differential entropy.**

This is why the Gaussian appears everywhere in nature. It is the distribution that makes the fewest assumptions (maximum disorder) while respecting a given energy (variance) constraint.

Generative AI Deep Learning

Entropy in Model Training

  • Variational Autoencoders (VAEs): During training, we force the latent variable \(z\) to follow a Unit Gaussian. We do this by minimizing the KL Divergence between the predicted latent PDF and the Gaussian PDF—effectively comparing their differential entropies.
  • Mutual Information Estimation: Measuring the relationship between two continuous variables (like an Image and its Embedding) requires calculating the difference between their joint and individual differential entropies.
  • Diffusion Models: These models work by slowly adding Gaussian noise to an image, maximizing its differential entropy until it becomes pure White Noise.
