1. Definition

Any square matrix \( A \) can be decomposed into:

$$ A = P L U $$
  • \( P \): Permutation matrix (records row swaps for stability).
  • \( L \): Lower triangular matrix (records elimination steps).
  • \( U \): Upper triangular matrix (the result of elimination coefficients).

2. Finding Det(A)

Since \( A = PLU \), then \( \det(A) = \det(P)\det(L)\det(U) \).

  • \( \det(P) \): +1 or -1 (depending on swaps).
  • \( \det(L) \): 1 (because diagonal is 1s).
  • \( \det(U) \): Product of diagonal entries (Pivots).

This is the standard, fast method to compute determinants.

3. SciPy LU

Extracting the matrices.

import numpy as np
import scipy.linalg as la

A = np.array([
    [2, 5, 8],
    [5, 2, 8],
    [7, 5, 6]
])

P, L, U = la.lu(A)

print("Permutation P:")
print(P)
print("\nLower L:")
print(L)
print("\nUpper U:")
print(U)

print("\nCheck (P @ L @ U):")
print(P @ L @ U)
