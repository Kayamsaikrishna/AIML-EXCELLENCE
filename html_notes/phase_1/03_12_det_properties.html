12. Properties of Determinants

Computational shortcuts for complex matrices.

1. Fundamental Rules

  • Product Rule: \( \text{det}(\mathbf{AB}) = \text{det}(\mathbf{A})\text{det}(\mathbf{B}) \).
  • Inverse Rule: \( \text{det}(\mathbf{A}^{-1}) = 1 / \text{det}(\mathbf{A}) \).
  • Transpose Rule: \( \text{det}(\mathbf{A}^T) = \text{det}(\mathbf{A}) \).
  • Scalar Rule: For an \( n \times n \) matrix, \( \text{det}(c\mathbf{A}) = c^n \text{det}(\mathbf{A}) \).

2. Special Structures

Triangular Matrices

The determinant of a triangular (or diagonal) matrix is simply the product of its diagonal elements.

$$ \text{det}(\mathbf{L}) = \prod_{i=1}^n l_{ii} $$

AI Use: This makes LU Decomposition extremely powerful for calculating determinants in log-probability calculations for models like GMMs.

3. Python: Verifying Properties

import numpy as np

A = np.random.rand(5, 5)
B = np.random.rand(5, 5)

# Property: det(AB) = det(A) * det(B)
det_AB = np.linalg.det(A @ B)
det_A_times_B = np.linalg.det(A) * np.linalg.det(B)

print(f"det(AB): {det_AB:.4f}")
print(f"det(A)*det(B): {det_A_times_B:.4f}")
print(f"Property holds: {np.isclose(det_AB, det_A_times_B)}")
