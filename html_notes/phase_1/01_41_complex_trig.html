41. Complex Numbers (Trigonometric Form)

Placing information on the Complex Plane.

1. Euler's Gift

Every complex number \( z = a + bi \) can be written as a Magnitude (\(r\)) and an Angle (\(\theta\)).

$$ z = r(\cos \theta + i \sin \theta) = r e^{i\theta} $$

Magnitude \(r\) is the "Strength" of the signal, and \(\theta\) is its "Phase".

2. Complex Valued Neural Networks (CVNNs)

Interference as a Logic Gate

Standard AI uses real numbers. But Complex Valued Networks allow neurons to perform calculations using phase interference. This is incredibly efficient for Medical Imaging (MRI) and Radio Frequency AI.

  • Rotary Attention (RoPE): As discussed earlier, RoPE is mathematically a complex multiplication by \( e^{i m \theta} \). It's simpler to implement in code using real numbers but the logic is pure 100% complex trig.
  • Stable Training: Complex weights can sometimes prevent the vanishing gradient problem by keeping the magnitude of activations bounded by the unit circle.

3. Python: Complex Rotations

# complex(a, b) creates a + bi
z1 = complex(1, 0) # Magnitude 1, Phase 0

# A rotation of 90 degrees (i)
rotation = complex(0, 1) # e^(i * pi/2)

z2 = z1 * rotation
print(f"Result: {z2}") 
# Output: (0+1j) -> The vector rotated exactly 90 degrees
