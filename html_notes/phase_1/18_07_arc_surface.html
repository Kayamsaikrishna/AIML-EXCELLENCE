07. Arc Length and Surface Area

Integrating the "Distance Formula".

1. Arc Length

The length of a curve from \(a\) to \(b\) is found by summing tiny segments using Pythagoras:

$$ L = \int_a^b \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} dx $$

2. Surface Area of Revolution

Rotating an arc around the x-axis:

$$ S = \int_a^b 2\pi f(x) \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} dx $$

3. AI Connection: Robotics Path Smoothing

When an AI plans a path for a robot, it generates a curved spline. However, the robot needs to know the Exact Distance it must travel to manage its battery or time. The Arc Length integral allows the AI to calculate the real-world distance of a virtual curve, ensuring the robot doesn't run out of power halfway through a "smooth" turn.

4. Python: Symbolic Arc Length

import sympy as sp

x = sp.Symbol('x')
f = x**(1.5) # x to the 3/2

# L = Integral of sqrt(1 + f'^2)
df = sp.diff(f, x)
integrand = sp.sqrt(1 + df**2)
length = sp.integrate(integrand, (x, 0, 4))

print(f"Arc length of {f} from x=0 to 4: {length}")
