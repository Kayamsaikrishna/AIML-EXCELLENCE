Stats 1.8 Inference

A/B Testing Foundations

The Scientific Method at Scale. How the world’s biggest companies decide which button to change.

The Simplest Experiment

An A/B test is simply a Two-Sample Hypothesis Test applied to product features. - A (Control): The current version of the product. - B (Treatment): The new proposed version.

Goal: Does version B yield a statistically significant improvement in our Key Performance Indicator (KPI)?

1. The Measurement Trio

1. Primary Metric

The ONE thing you want to move (e.g., Conversion Rate or Click-Through Rate).

2. Secondary Metric

Things that might also change (e.g., Average Order Value). Gives context to the primary metric.

3. Guardrail Metric

The "Don't Break This" metric (e.g., Latency or Error Rate). If clicks go up but errors double, you don't launch!

2. The Statistics of Launching

$$ H_0: \mu_B - \mu_A = 0 \quad (\text{No effect}) $$ $$ H_1: \mu_B - \mu_A \neq 0 \quad (\text{Effect exists}) $$
- Alpha (\(\alpha\)): Usually 0.05. The probability of calling it a "win" when it was just luck. - P-Value: The probability of seeing our results if the Null Hypothesis were true.

3. ML Connection: Model Deployment

Product ML Monitoring

Why we A/B test Neural Networks

In ML, "Offline" metrics (like RMSE or Accuracy on training data) often lie about real-world performance.

  • The Offline-Online Gap: Your new model might have 10% better accuracy, but because it's 200ms slower, users bounce before seeing the result. A/B testing captures the UX impact of your model.
  • Canary Deployments: A "Soft" A/B test. You route 1% of traffic to the new model (B) and check for errors or massive drops in metrics before ramping up to 50/50.
  • Novelty Effect: Users often click new things just because they are new. A/B tests need to run long enough (usually 14 days) to allow the "excitement" to wear off and see the true long-term behavior.
Engineer's Fact: You cannot check your A/B test result every hour and stop when it looks good. This is called "P-hacking" or "Peeking". It artificially inflates your False Positive rate by 3-5x!
