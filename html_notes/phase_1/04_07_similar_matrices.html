1. Definition

Two matrices \( A \) and \( B \) are similar if there exists an invertible matrix \( P \) such that:

$$ B = P^{-1} A P $$

Crucial Property: Similar matrices share the same:

  • Eigenvalues.
  • Determinant.
  • Trace.
  • Rank.

2. Feature Transformations

When we train a neural network layer, we are often learning a transformation \( A \).

Sometimes, we "whiten" the data (change basis) before the layer. The operation performed in the new basis \( B \) is mathematically similar to \( A \). The underlying "action" (like rotation or scaling) remains identical despite the coordinate shift.

3. Verifying Invariants

import numpy as np

A = np.array([[1, 2], 
              [3, 4]])

# Random Change of Basis P
P = np.array([[2, 5], 
              [1, 3]])
P_inv = np.linalg.inv(P)

# B is similar to A
B = P_inv @ A @ P

print("A:\n", A)
print("\nB (Similar):\n", B.round(2))

# Check Invariants
print(f"\nTrace A: {np.trace(A)}, Trace B: {np.trace(B):.2f}")
print(f"Det A: {np.linalg.det(A):.2f}, Det B: {np.linalg.det(B):.2f}")

evals_A = np.sort(np.linalg.eigvals(A))
evals_B = np.sort(np.linalg.eigvals(B))
print(f"Eigenvals A: {evals_A.round(4)}")
print(f"Eigenvals B: {evals_B.round(4)}")
# Should match exactly
