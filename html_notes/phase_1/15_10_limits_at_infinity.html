10. Limits at Infinity

Long-term behavior and Horizontal Asymptotes.

1. Definition

What happens to \(f(x)\) as \(x\) grows large without bound?

$$ \lim_{x \to \infty} f(x) = L $$

If this exists, \(y=L\) is a Horizontal Asymptote.

2. Rational Functions Strategy

To evaluate \(\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\), divide every term by the highest power of \(x\) in the denominator.

$$ \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{2x^2 + 1}{x^2 - 4} = \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{2 + 1/x^2}{1 - 4/x^2} = 2 $$

3. AI Connection: Learning to Converge

The Asymptotic Optimizer

When we say an algorithm is "consistent", we mean that its error goes to zero in the limit as we get infinite data. $$ \lim_{n \to \infty} E[\hat{\theta}_n - \theta] = 0 $$ This is the ultimate goal in statistical learning: ensuring that with enough experience, the AI's understanding perfectly matches reality.

4. Python: Symbolic Asymptotes

import sympy as sp

x = sp.Symbol('x')
f = (2*x**2 + 5) / (x**2 - 3)

# Limit at infinity
L = sp.limit(f, x, sp.oo)

print(f"The horizontal asymptote is y = {L}")
