1. Definition

For any matrix \( A = U \Sigma V^T \), the Pseudoinverse \( A^+ \) is:

$$ A^+ = V \Sigma^+ U^T $$

Where \( \Sigma^+ \) is formed by taking the reciprocal of non-zero singular values and transposing.

2. Linear Regression

The solution to Ordinary Least Squares (finding weights \( w \) to minimize \( ||Xw - y||^2 \)) is:

$$ w = (X^T X)^{-1} X^T y $$

This \( (X^T X)^{-1} X^T \) term is the Pseudoinverse of \( X \) (specifically clearly defined when columns are independent). In practice, we just calculate \( w = X^+ y \).

3. NumPy pinv

import numpy as np

# A "Tall" matrix (Overdetermined system)
# 3 equations, 2 unknowns
X = np.array([
    [1, 1],
    [1, 2],
    [1, 3]
])
y = np.array([6, 9, 12]) 
# y = 3x + 3 roughly

# Solve using Pseudoinverse
X_pinv = np.linalg.pinv(X)
w = X_pinv @ y

print("Weights (intercept, slope):")
print(w.round(2))

# Verify with Least Squares
w_lstsq, res, rank, s = np.linalg.lstsq(X, y, rcond=None)
print("\nNumPy lstsq weights:")
print(w_lstsq.round(2))
