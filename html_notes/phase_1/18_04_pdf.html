Prob 1.4 Density

Probability Density Function (PDF)

Probability per unit length.

Density != Probability

For continuous variables, \(f_X(x)\) represents the density at point \(x\). To get probability, you must integrate area under the curve! \[ P(a < X < b)=\int_{a}^{b} f_X(x) dx \] Properties:

  1. \(f_X(x) \ge 0\) (Can be > 1!)
  2. \(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_X(x) dx = 1\) (Total Area = 1)

2. ML Connection: Regularization

Priors as PDFs

When we say "Assume a Gaussian Prior on weights", we mean the probability density of the weights follows the bell curve equation: \[ p(w) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-w^2/2} \] Maximizing this density (MAP Estimate) pushes \(w\) towards 0, which is exactly L2 Regularization.
