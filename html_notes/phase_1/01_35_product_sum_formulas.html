1. Multiplying Sinusoids

Formula that turns multiplication into addition:

$$ \sin(A)\sin(B) = \frac{1}{2} [\cos(A-B) - \cos(A+B)] $$

This says: Multiplying two frequencies creates two NEW frequencies: the Sum and the Difference.

2. Feature Interaction

In Neural Networks, a "GLU" (Gated Linear Unit) or Dot Product Attention essentially multiplies signals.

By determining the product of two features, the model learns the interaction between them. Just like AM Radio embeds voice onto a carrier wave, AI embeds context onto word vectors via multiplication.

3. Beat Frequencies

Hearing the difference between two close notes.

import numpy as np

# Two frequencies very close together
f1 = 440 # A4 Note
f2 = 445 # Slightly sharp

# Their product creates a Sum (885Hz) and Diff (5Hz)
# The 5Hz difference is heard as a "Wah-Wah-Wah" pulsing beat.

print(f"Mixing {f1}Hz and {f2}Hz...")
print(f"Resulting Beat Frequency: {abs(f1-f2)} Hz")
print("This pulses 5 times a second.")
