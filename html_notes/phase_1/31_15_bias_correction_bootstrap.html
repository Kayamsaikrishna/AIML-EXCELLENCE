15. Bias Correction via Bootstrap

Correcting for systematic "Leans" in our estimates.

1. What is Estimation Bias?

An estimator $\hat{\theta}$ is Biased if its expected value is not equal to the true parameter: $E[\hat{\theta}] \ne \theta$. Some very common estimators, like the maximum likelihood estimator of variance, are systematically biased (they tend to underestimate the truth).

2. The Correction Formula

The Bootstrap allows us to estimate this bias by comparing our original estimate to the average of our bootstrap simulations.

$$ \widehat{\text{Bias}} = \bar{\theta}^* - \hat{\theta} $$ $$ \hat{\theta}_{\text{Corrected}} = \hat{\theta} - \widehat{\text{Bias}} = 2\hat{\theta} - \bar{\theta}^* $$

By subtracting the estimated bias, we move the estimator closer to the true value of the population parameter.

3. AI/ML Connection: Model Calibration and Risk Assessment

Beyond Over-Fitting

In AI, Bias Correction is the key to Calibration.

A classifier might be 90% accurate, but it might be Confident-Biased—it predicts "High Probability" for cases that are actually ambiguous. By bootstrapping the validation set and calculating the bias between predicted probabilities and actual outcomes, we can Calibrate the model. This is critical for Financial Risk AI** or Medical Screening. If an AI predicts a 10% risk of a market crash, but we know the model has a +2% bootstrap bias, we correct it to 8%. This ensures that the "Business Logic" of the AI is not just optimized for the training data, but is mathematically adjusted to be "Fair" and "Truthful" in its self-assessment.

4. Python: Correcting the Variance Bias

# Example: Population Var is 25. Small samples often underestimate it.
data = np.random.normal(50, 5, 10)
raw_var = np.var(data) # Biased (n instead of n-1)

boot_vars = [np.var(np.random.choice(data, 10)) for _ in range(5000)]
bias_est = np.mean(boot_vars) - raw_var
corrected_var = raw_var - bias_est

print(f"Raw Estimate: {raw_var:.2f}")
print(f"Bootstrap Corrected: {corrected_var:.2f}")
# The corrected value will likely be closer to 25.0
