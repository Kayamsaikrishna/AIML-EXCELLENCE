Graph Theory Capstone

Graph Theory: The Synthesis

Connecting the World. Summarizing how the mathematical study of connections forms the structural nervous system of modern artificial intelligence.

The Structural Prior

If Combinatorics is the math of selection, Graph Theory is the math of Structure. In AI, we use graphs to represent everything from the physical layout of a GPU to the abstract relationship between words in a sentence. mastering graphs is mastering the medium of intelligence.

1. The Graph Theory Stack

Foundations

Vertices, Edges, and Degrees. The Handshaking Lemma (\(\sum d(v) = 2|E|\)) provides our first fundamental constraint on network complexity.

Connectivity

Paths, Cycles, and Components. Ensuring information flow and avoiding feedback loops (DAGs) in training pipelines.

Hierarchies (Trees)

Minimal connectivity. Decision Trees and Parse Trees provide the most human-interpretable models in the AI arsenal.
