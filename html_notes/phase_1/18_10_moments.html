Prob 1.4 The Shape

Moments

Characterizing the distribution.

The Hierarchy

Moments describe the "shape" of a distribution using powers of the variable. The \(n\)-th moment is \(E[X^n]\). The \(n\)-th central moment is \(E[(X-\mu)^n]\).

  1. 1st Moment: Mean (Location).
  2. 2nd Moment: Variance (Spread).
  3. 3rd Moment: Skewness (Asymmetry).
  4. 4th Moment: Kurtosis (Tail thickness).

2. ML Connection: Adam Optimizer

Estimating Moments

The Adam (Adaptive Moment Estimation) optimizer works by keeping running estimates of the 1st and 2nd moments of the gradients! \[ m_t \approx E[\nabla L] \quad \text{(Mean of gradients)} \] \[ v_t \approx E[\nabla L^2] \quad \text{(Uncentered Variance of gradients)} \] It uses these to adapt the learning rate for each parameter.
