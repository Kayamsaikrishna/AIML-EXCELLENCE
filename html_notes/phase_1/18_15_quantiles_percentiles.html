Quantiles & Percentiles

Slicing the distribution.

Definition

The \(p\)-th quantile \(Q(p)\) is the value below which \(p\) percent of the data falls. \(F_X(Q(p)) = p\).

  • 25th Percentile: 1st Quartile (Q1)
  • 50th Percentile: Median (Q2)
  • 75th Percentile: 3rd Quartile (Q3)
IQR (Interquartile Range): \(Q3 - Q1\). Measure of spread robust to outliers.

2. ML Connection: Box Plots & Outliers

Quantile Loss

In Quantile Regression, we don't just predict the mean. We predict the confidence intervals (e.g., 90th percentile). This is critical for forecasting (e.g., Stock prices, Inventory) where we need to know the "worst-case" scenario, not just the "average" scenario.
