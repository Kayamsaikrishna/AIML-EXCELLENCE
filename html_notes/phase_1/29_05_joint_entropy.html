Info Theory Multivariate

Joint Entropy

Measuring the combined uncertainty of a multi-variable system. How much information is contained in the pair (X, Y)?

The Ensemble Uncertainty

In a single-variable system, we measure the surprise of one event. In a joint system, we measure the surprise of seeing a specific combination of events.

If \(X\) is "Rain" and \(Y\) is "Traffic Jam," joint entropy measures the total uncertainty of the weather and traffic system as a whole.

1. Definition

For a pair of discrete random variables \((X, Y)\) with joint probability distribution \(P(x, y)\):

$$ H(X, Y) = -\sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} \sum_{y \in \mathcal{Y}} P(x, y) \log_2 P(x, y) $$

This is simply the Shannon entropy of the joint distribution. It quantifies the number of bits required to describe the entire system state.

2. The Independence Inequality

A crucial rule of thumb:

$$ H(X, Y) \le H(X) + H(Y) $$
  • Equality (\(=\)): Happens if and only if \(X\) and \(Y\) are independent. The total uncertainty is just the sum of individual parts.
  • **Less Than (\(<\)):** Happens if \(X\) and \(Y\) are **correlated** or dependent. Because they share information, you need *fewer* bits to describe them together than if you described them separately.

3. Visualization: Information Overlap

Think of entropy as areas. If the circles for \(X\) and \(Y\) overlap, the "Total Area" (Joint Entropy) is less than the sum of the two circles.

The overlap region is what we will later call Mutual Information. The joint entropy is the outer boundary of both circles.

Computer Vision Multimodal Learning

Joint Entropy in Modern AI

  • Feature Correlation: If two features in your dataset have a joint entropy nearly equal to the entropy of just one, it means they are highly redundant. One can be removed without losing information.
  • Contrastive Learning (e.g., CLIP): Models like CLIP try to align Image (\(X\)) and Text (\(Y\)). By maximizing the similarity of their embeddings, they are effectively trying to minimize the joint entropy of the pair—making the combination highly predictable and related.
  • Multi-variable Optimization: When training a neural network with multiple heads (e.g., predicting both Object Class and Bounding Box), the joint entropy of the targets helps us understand the fundamental difficulty of the task.

4. Chain Rule (Preview)

Joint entropy can also be broken down sequentially:

$$ H(X, Y) = H(X) + H(Y|X) $$

This says: "The total uncertainty of the system is the uncertainty of \(X\), plus the remaining uncertainty of \(Y\) once you already know \(X\)."
