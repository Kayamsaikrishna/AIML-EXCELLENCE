Stats 1.5 Interactions

ANOVA (Two-Way)

Studying how two different categorical variables affect a single outcome, and whether they work together.

Beyond One Factor

A Two-way ANOVA answers three questions at once: 1. Main Effect of Factor A: Does A matter? 2. Main Effect of Factor B: Does B matter? 3. Interaction Effect (A x B): Does the effect of A depend on the level of B?

2. ML Connection: Hyperparameter Tuning

Deep Learning Ops

The Optimization Matrix

You are researching the best training setup. - Factor A (Optimizer): SGD, Adam, RMSProp. - Factor B (Learning Rate): 1e-1, 1e-2, 1e-3.

You train 3 runs for every combination (3x3 grid = 9 cells, 27 runs total). A Two-Way ANOVA tells you: - "Adam is generally better than SGD (Main Effect A)." - "1e-2 is generally better than 1e-1 (Main Effect B)." - CRITICAL: "Adam performs best at 1e-3, while SGD performs best at 1e-1 (Interaction Effect)."

Finding the interaction effect is the key to deep learning success, as hyperparameters rarely work in isolation.

3. Visualizing Interaction

If the lines on a plot of Group Means are Parallel, there is NO interaction. If the lines Cross or diverge, an interaction exists.
