Inverse Functions & Composition

Chains of Reason. Combining simple mappings to build complex logic and finding the way back home through the power of inversion.

The Feedback Loop

Functions are the building blocks of algorithms. Composition is the act of piping output to input. Inverses are the act of undoing a calculation. Together, they define the flow and recoverability of information.

1. Function Composition (\(f \circ g\))

Applying one function after another. The range of the first must be a subset of the domain of the second.

\( (f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x)) \)

Note: Composition is NOT commutative. \(f(g(x)) \neq g(f(x))\) in most cases. The order matters!

2. Inverse Functions (\(f^{-1}\))

A function \(f^{-1}\) that "undoes" \(f\). If \(f\) maps \(x\) to \(y\), then \(f^{-1}\) maps \(y\) back to \(x\).

\( f^{-1}(f(x)) = x \text{ and } f(f^{-1}(y)) = y \)

Existence

A function has an inverse if and only if it is Bijective (One-to-One and Onto).

Symmetry

The graph of \(f^{-1}\) is the reflection of the graph of \(f\) across the line \(y = x\).

Deep Learning Encryption

Why Logic matters in AI

  • The Chain Rule: Backpropagation (the engine of Deep Learning) is practically an application of the derivative of composed functions. By chaining layers, we build complex mappings, and by using the chain rule, we flow gradients backward.
  • Reversible Computing: Some neural architectures (like RevNets) use strictly invertible functions for each layer. This allows them to store only the final output and "uncompute" layers in reverse to save memory during training.
  • Cryptography: RSA encryption relies on the fact that while some functions are easy to compute (multiplying two large primes), their inverses (factoring the result) are practically impossible. This is a "One-Way Function."
  • Word Embeddings: When we perform "Vector Arithmetic" like *King - Man + Woman = Queen*, we are essentially traversing a composition of relations in the vector space.
