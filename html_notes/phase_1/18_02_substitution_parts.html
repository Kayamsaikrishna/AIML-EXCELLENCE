02. U-Substitution and Integration by Parts

The inverse of nesting and interaction.

1. U-Substitution (Reverse Chain Rule)

If you have an integral of the form \(\int f(g(x))g'(x)dx\), let \(u = g(x)\) then \(du = g'(x)dx\):

$$ \int f(u) du $$

Goal: Transform a messy integral into a basic one.

2. Integration by Parts (Reverse Product Rule)

Derived from \(\frac{d}{dx}(uv) = u\frac{dv}{dx} + v\frac{du}{dx}\):

$$ \int u \, dv = uv - \int v \, du $$

Mnemonic: LIATE (Log, Inverse trig, Algebraic, Trig, Exponential) to choose \(u\).

3. AI Connection: Gaussian Integrals

The Normal Distribution

In Probability, the Gaussian function \(e^{-x^2}\) cannot be integrated with simple antiderivatives. However, variations like \(\int x e^{-x^2} dx\) are solved instantly using U-Substitution (\(u = -x^2\)). Integration by parts is used to derive the Variance of the normal distribution, a fundamental parameter in every Machine Learning model from Linear Regression to VAEs.

4. Python: Symbolic Step-by-Step

import sympy as sp

x = sp.Symbol('x')

# Integration by Parts: Integral of x * exp(x)
u = x
dv = sp.exp(x)
# Result should be uv - int(v du) = x*exp(x) - exp(x)

res = sp.integrate(x * sp.exp(x), x)
print(f"Integral of x*e^x: {res}")
