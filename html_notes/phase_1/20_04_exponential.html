Prob 1.4 The Decay

The Exponential Distribution

Modeling the time between independent events.

The Continuous Wait

The Exponential distribution is the continuous sibling of the Geometric distribution. While Geometric models the number of *discrete* trials, Exponential models the *amount* of time or space.

It is the only continuous distribution with the Memoryless Property.

1. Definition and PDF

A Random Variable \(X\) follows an Exponential distribution with rate parameter \(\lambda > 0\): $$ X \sim \text{Exp}(\lambda) $$ The Probability Density Function (PDF) is: $$ f(x) = \begin{cases} \lambda e^{-\lambda x} & x \ge 0 \\ 0 & x < 0 \end{cases} $$

Where \(\lambda\) is the average number of events per unit time (same as Poisson).

2. Cumulative Distribution Function (CDF)

The probability that an event happens before time \(x\):

$$ F(x) = P(X \le x) = 1 - e^{-\lambda x} $$

Survival Function: The probability that an event has NOT happened yet by time \(x\): $$ S(x) = P(X > x) = e^{-\lambda x} $$

3. Moments

  • Mean: \(E[X] = \frac{1}{\lambda}\).
    If buses arrive at a rate of 2 per hour (\(\lambda=2\)), the average wait is 0.5 hours.
  • Variance: \(\text{Var}(X) = \frac{1}{\lambda^2}\).

4. The Memoryless Property

$$ P(X > s + t \mid X > s) = P(X > t) $$

Deep Intuition: Suppose you are waiting for a radioactive atom to decay. If you've already waited 100 years and it hasn't decayed, the probability that it decays in the next 5 minutes is exactly the same as if you had just started watching it.

Real World vs. Theory: Human mortality is NOT memoryless (we age). If you are 80, the probability of living another 20 years is much lower than if you are 20. But for technical failures like lightbulbs or computer parts (before they start wearing out), the Exponential model is a decent approximation.

5. ML Connection: Survival Analysis

Predictive Maintenance Churn Prediction

Modeling Time-to-Event

In ML, we often predict the time until an event occurs:

  • Churn: How many days until a user cancels their subscription?
  • Finance: How long until a loan defaults?
  • Health: How long until a patient relapses?

The Exponential distribution is the baseline model for these tasks. However, its memoryless property is often too restrictive, leading to the use of the Weibull Distribution (coming later), which allows the failure rate to increase or decrease over time.
