Prob 1.4 Spread

Variance

Measuring the chaos.

Definition

Variance measures how far a set of numbers are spread out from their average value. \[ Var(X) = E[(X - \mu)^2] \] Practical computation formula: \[ Var(X) = E[X^2] - (E[X])^2 \] It is sensitive to outliers (due to squaring).

2. ML Connection: Bias-Variance Tradeoff

Overfitting vs Underfitting

The error of an ML model can be decomposed into: \[ \text{Error} = \text{Bias}^2 + \text{Variance} + \text{Irreducible Noise} \]

  • High Bias: Model is too simple (Underfitting). Cannot capture the pattern.
  • High Variance: Model is too complex (Overfitting). Captures the noise.
