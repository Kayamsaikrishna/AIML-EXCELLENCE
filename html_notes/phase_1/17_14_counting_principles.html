Prob 1.4 Combinatorics

Counting Principles

How large is the space?

Multiplication Principle

If experiment 1 has \(n_1\) outcomes and experiment 2 has \(n_2\) outcomes, the total number of combined outcomes is \(n_1 \times n_2\).

This explains why high-dimensional spaces are huge. A 100x100 binary image has \(2^{10,000}\) possible states. This is Combinatorial Explosion.

2. ML Connection: Grid Search

Hyperparameter Space

Searching for optimal hyperparameters is a counting problem. If you have 3 params with 5, 5, and 10 options respectively, you have \(5 \times 5 \times 10 = 250\) combinations to test. Adding one more param with 10 options makes it 2500. This is why we prefer Random Search or Bayesian Optimization over exhaustive Grid Search.
