06. Triple Integrals

Integrating across 3D volumes.

1. The Concept

A triple integral \(\iiint_{E} f(x, y, z) dV\) sums up a property (like density or probability) over a 3D region \(E\). If \(f(x, y, z) = 1\), the integral gives the Volume of the region.

$$ V = \iiint_{E} dV = \int_{a}^{b} \int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)} \int_{u_1(x,y)}^{u_2(x,y)} dz dy dx $$

2. The Order of Integration

There are 6 possible orders to evaluate a triple integral (\(dz dy dx\), \(dx dy dz\), etc.). Choosing the right order can often simplify the mathematical complexity significantly, especially when the boundaries of the volume are complex.

3. AI/ML Connection: Volumetric Rendering & Segmentation

Voxel Data & Medical AI

Modern AI (like NeRFs or Medical Segmentation models) doesn't just see "Pixels" (2D); it sees "Voxels" (3D pixels). To calculate the "Occupancy" or "Opacity" of a region in 3D space, an AI model effectively performs a Triple Integral of its density predictions.

This is crucial for Autonomous Driving (detecting if a voxel is occupied by a car or is empty air) and Cancer Detection (calculating the precise volume of a tumor from a CT scan). Without triple integration, AI couldn't bridge the gap between 2D images and the 3D reality of the world.

4. Python: Triple Integration for Volume

from scipy.integrate import tplquad

# Volume of a box [0, 1] x [0, 2] x [0, 3]
# Result: 1 * 2 * 3 = 6

f = lambda z, y, x: 1
volume, error = tplquad(f, 0, 1, lambda x: 0, lambda x: 2, 
                         lambda x, y: 0, lambda x, y: 3)

print(f"Volume of the box: {volume:.4f}")
