15. Permutations

When order matters.

1. Order and Selection

A Permutation is an arrangement of objects in a specific order. If you change the order, you have a different permutation.

2. The Formulas

  • Without Repetition: Choosing \(r\) items from \(n\) distinct items. $$ P(n, r) = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!} $$
  • With Repetition: Choosing \(r\) items from \(n\) types where items can be reused. $$ n^r $$

3. AI/ML Connection: Genetic Algorithms

Pathfinding and Optimization

In Evolutionary AI and Genetic Algorithms, a "Chromosome" is often represented as a permutation of genes.

For problems like the Traveling Salesperson Problem (TSP)—which AI uses to optimize delivery routes—the sample space is the set of all possible permutations of cities. If there are 10 cities, there are $10! = 3,628,800$ possible routes. Permutations allow AI Researchers to understand the Search Complexity of discrete problems. Understanding how to swap and mutate these permutations without simple repetition is key to "Evolving" better solutions for complex logistics and scheduling.

4. Python: Calculating Permutations

import math

n = 10 # Total items
r = 3  # Items chosen

# Pure Math Formula
p_n_r = math.factorial(n) / math.factorial(n - r)

print(f"Permutations P({n}, {r}): {p_n_r}")

# Python Built-in Generator
from itertools import permutations
items = ['A', 'B', 'C']
print(f"Permutations of {items}: {list(permutations(items))}")
