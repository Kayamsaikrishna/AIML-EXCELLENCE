Truth Tables & Logical Equivalence

Mapping the Possibilities. Systematic tools for analyzing every possible world and proving that two complex statements are secretly identical.

The Brute Force of Truth

A Truth Table is a matrix that lists every possible combination of truth values for its components. If a statement has \(n\) variables, the table has \(2^n\) rows. It is the exhaustive "simulation" of a logical system.

1. Analyzing Compound Statements

Let's analyze the implication \(p \rightarrow q\) and its contrapositive \(\neg q \rightarrow \neg p\).

p q ¬p ¬q p → q ¬q → ¬p
T T F F T T
T F F T F F
F T T F T T
F F T T T T

Since the last two columns are identical, we say they are Logically Equivalent (\(\equiv\)).

2. Tautologies, Contradictions, and Contingencies

Tautology

A statement that is always true, no matter what. Example: \(p \lor \neg p\) (The Law of Excluded Middle).

Contradiction

A statement that is always false. Example: \(p \land \neg p\) (The Law of Non-Contradiction).

Contingency

A statement that is true in some cases and false in others. Most natural language falls here.

3. De Morgan's Laws

Fundamental rules for distributing "NOT" across "AND" and "OR":

$$ \neg(p \land q) \equiv \neg p \lor \neg q $$ $$ \neg(p \lor q) \equiv \neg p \land \neg q $$

Intuition: "I don't have (Apples AND Bananas)" means "I (Don't have Apples) OR (Don't have Bananas)."

Constraint Satisfaction SAT Solvers

The Complexity of Truth

While truth tables are easy for 2 or 3 variables, they become impossible for thousands. This is the SAT (Boolean Satisfiability) Problem.

  • Configuration AI: When you customize a computer online (e.g., Apple Store), an underlying SAT solver uses logical equivalence and truth analysis to ensure you don't pick a motherboard that is incompatible with your RAM.
  • Circuit Design: Hardware engineers use De Morgan's Laws to simplify massive logic circuits, reducing the number of physical transistors needed while keeping the "Truth Table" identical.
  • Neuro-symbolic AI: Modern research tries to combine Deep Learning with SAT solvers to create AI that can both "feel" patterns (Neural) and "prove" its answers (Symbolic).
