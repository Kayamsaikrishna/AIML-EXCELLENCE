20. Inference in BNs & AI Applications

Turning graphs into decisions.

1. Inference: Asking questions

Once a Bayesian Network is built, we perform Inference to get answers.

  • Exact Inference (Variable Elimination): Mathematically exact but computationally slow for large graphs.
  • Approximate Inference (MCMC/Sampling): Scaling to massive systems by simulating paths through the graph.

2. The "Active" Bayesian

Bayesian AI doesn't just predict; it acts. By combining the Posterior with a **Utility Function**, we enter the world of Decision Theory (e.g., "Expected Utility"). AI makes the choice that is most likely to yield the highest reward given its current uncertainty.

3. AI/ML Connection: The Future of Probabilistic Programming

The Grand Unified Theory

The crown jewel of Bayesian Statistics in AI is Probabilistic Programming Platforms** like **PyMC**, **Edward**, and **Pyro**.

These tools allow AI researchers to define complex physical and social models as Bayesian Networks and "Compile" them into Neural Networks. This is the path to Neuro-Symbolic AI: combining the raw power of Deep Learning with the logical rigor of Bayesian probability. From **Climate Change forecasting** to **Deep Space navigation**, Bayesian inference is the "Trust layer" of AI. It ensures that machines don't just find patterns, but understand the **Uncertainty** and **Causality** of the universe they inhabit.
