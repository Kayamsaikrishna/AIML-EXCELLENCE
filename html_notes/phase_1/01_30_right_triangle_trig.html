1. Breaking Vectors Down

Any movement in 2D space (Hypotenuse \( r \)) can be broken into two orthogonal movements:

  • Horizontal: \( x = r \cos(\theta) \) (Projection onto X)
  • Vertical: \( y = r \sin(\theta) \) (Projection onto Y)

In Physics Game Engines (Unity/Unreal) and AI Robotics, this is how we update movement.

2. The Foundation of Search

The Dot Product is intimately related to the Cosine of the angle.

$$ A \cdot B = \|A\| \|B\| \cos(\theta) $$

If we normalize vectors (\( \|A\|=1, \|B\|=1 \)), then \( A \cdot B = \cos(\theta) \). This makes "Right Triangle Trig" the core algorithm for finding similar documents in RAG systems.

3. Decomposing Movement

Calculating velocity components.

import math

speed = 10.0 # Hypotenuse (m/s)
angle_deg = 30.0
angle_rad = math.radians(angle_deg)

# Decompose into x (Adjacent) and y (Opposite)
vx = speed * math.cos(angle_rad)
vy = speed * math.sin(angle_rad)

print(f"Velocity Vector: [{vx:.2f}, {vy:.2f}]")
# Check Pythagoras
check_speed = math.sqrt(vx**2 + vy**2)
print(f"Verified Speed: {check_speed:.2f}")
