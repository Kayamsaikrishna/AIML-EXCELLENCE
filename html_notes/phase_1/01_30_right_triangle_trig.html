30. Right Triangle Trigonometry

The Geometry of Orthogonality.

1. Pythagorean Foundations

Every right triangle follows the rule that defines Euclidean Distance:

$$ a^2 + b^2 = c^2 $$

In terms of Trig Functions:

  • Side Opposite (\(O\)) = \(H \sin \theta\)
  • Side Adjacent (\(A\)) = \(H \cos \theta\)

2. Computer Vision & Engineering

Orthogonal Projection

In 3D Computer Vision, we often need to project a 3D point onto a 2D camera plane. This is pure Right Triangle Trig.

  • Ray Casting: To determine if a pixel is occupied, we cast a ray and use \( \sin \) and \( \cos \) to check intersections.
  • L2 Norm: The standard Euclidean distance used in loss functions is just the Hypotenuse calculation scaled to \( n \) dimensions.

3. Python: Euclidean Distance

import math

# A triangle with base 3 and height 4 (The 3-4-5 Triangle)
base = 3
height = 4

# Pythagorean calculation
hypotenuse = math.sqrt(base**2 + height**2)

# Finding the angle (Inverse Trig)
theta_rad = math.atan2(height, base)
theta_deg = math.degrees(theta_rad)

print(f"Hypotenuse: {hypotenuse}") # 5.0
print(f"Angle: {theta_deg:.2f}°")   # 53.13°
