03. Vector Addition & Subtraction

Combining signal and noise.

1. Algebraic & Geometric Addition

Vectors are added component-wise. Geometrically, this follows the Triangle Law or the Parallelogram Law.

$$ \mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v} = \begin{bmatrix} u_1 + v_1 \\ u_2 + v_2 \end{bmatrix} $$

2. Residual Connections (Skip Connections)

The ResNet Trick

One of the most important discoveries in Deep Learning is the Residual Connection. Instead of learning a transformation \( y = f(x) \), the network learns the "difference" \( y = f(x) + x \).

  • Vector Addition: By adding the original input vector back to the output, gradients flow easily through very deep networks.
  • Information Preservation: Subtraction (\( y - x \)) allows us to analyze what features were actually added by a specific layer.

3. Python: Vector Arithmetic

import numpy as np

u = np.array([2, -5])
v = np.array([3, 2])

sum_vec = u + v      # Output: [5, -3]
diff_vec = u - v     # Output: [-1, -7]

print(f"Sum: {sum_vec}")
print(f"Difference: {diff_vec}")
