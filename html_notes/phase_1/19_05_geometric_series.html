05. Geometric Series

The foundation of infinite summation.

1. The Formula

A Geometric Series is a series where each term is a constant multiple of the previous term. It has the form:

$$ S = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} ar^n = a + ar + ar^2 + ar^3 + \dots $$

The series converges if \(|r| < 1\) and its sum is:

$$ S = \frac{a}{1 - r} $$

2. Why it matters

If \(|r| \geq 1\), the terms grow or stay the same, meaning the sum diverges to infinity. This is the most famous series because it is one of the few where we can calculate the exact sum easily.

3. AI/ML Connection: The Infinite Future

Reinforcement Learning Rewards

In Reinforcement Learning (RL), an AI agent receives rewards over time. But a reward today is worth more than a reward tomorrow. We calculate the "Return" as a Discounted Sum:

$$ G_t = R_{t+1} + \gamma R_{t+2} + \gamma^2 R_{t+3} + \dots $$

Here, \(\gamma\) (the discount factor) acts exactly like the common ratio \(r\). To ensure the AI doesn't see "infinite" value in just standing still, we set \(\gamma < 1\). The Geometric Series formula allows RL researchers to prove that the total expected value is finite, which is what makes training algorithms like Q-Learning mathematically sound.

4. Python: Calculating Geometric Sums

# a = 1, r = 0.5. Sum should be 1 / (1 - 0.5) = 2.0
a = 1
r = 0.5

# Numerical approximation
partial_sum = sum(a * (r**n) for n in range(100))
# Theoretical formula
theoretical_sum = a / (1 - r)

print(f"Partial Sum (100 terms): {partial_sum:.10f}")
print(f"Theoretical Sum: {theoretical_sum:.10f}")
