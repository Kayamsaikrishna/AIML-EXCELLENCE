33. Sum & Difference Formulas

Rotating space through the addition of angles.

1. The Addition Rules

$$ \sin(A \pm B) = \sin A \cos B \pm \cos A \sin B $$ $$ \cos(A \pm B) = \cos A \cos B \mp \sin A \sin B $$ $$ \tan(A \pm B) = \frac{\tan A \pm \tan B}{1 \mp \tan A \tan B} $$

2. Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE)

The Heart of Modern LLMs

Models like Llama and Mistral use RoPE to inject position information. Instead of adding a fixed vector, they rotate the query and key vectors. The math behind these rotations is a direct application of Sum/Difference formulas.

  • Relative Position: By rotating vectors, the dot product (Attention score) between two tokens only depends on the difference between their positions (\(m-n\)).
  • Formula Application: \( \cos(m\theta - n\theta) \) is calculated using these identities to determine affinity between distant tokens.

3. Python: Vector Rotation

import numpy as np

def rotate_v(v, theta):
    """ Rotates a 2D vector using trig formulas. """
    x, y = v
    # x' = x cos θ - y sin θ
    # y' = x sin θ + y cos θ
    new_x = x * np.cos(theta) - y * np.sin(theta)
    new_y = x * np.sin(theta) + y * np.cos(theta)
    return np.array([new_x, new_y])

v = np.array([1, 0])
rotated_45 = rotate_v(v, np.pi/4)

print(f"Original: {v}")
print(f"Rotated 45°: {rotated_45}") 
# Output: [0.707, 0.707] (1/√2)
