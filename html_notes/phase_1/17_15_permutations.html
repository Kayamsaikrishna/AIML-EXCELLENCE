Prob 1.4 Order Matters

Permutations

Arranging items where sequence counts.

The Factorial

The number of ways to arrange \(n\) distinct items is \(n!\) ("n factorial"). If selecting \(k\) items from \(n\): \[ P(n, k) = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!} \]

2. ML Connection: Sequence Models

Order Matters

In NLP (Natural Language Processing), "Dog bites Man" is different from "Man bites Dog". The permutation of words carries meaning. This is why Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and Transformers use Positional Encodings to respect the order of the inputs. Bag-of-Words models (which ignore permutations) lose this context.
