Stats 1.6 Real World AI

Bayesian Inference Applications

Where the math meets the metal. How modern AI uses Bayesian logic to build safer, smarter systems.

The Predictive Posterior

The ultimate application of Bayes isn't finding parameters \(\theta\); it's predicting **New Data** (\(X_{new}\)).

$$ P(x_{new} | D) = \int P(x_{new} | \theta) P(\theta | D) d\theta $$

Instead of using one "best" model, we make a prediction that is an Average of every possible model, weighted by how much we trust them. This is the Gold Standard for forecasting.

1. Key Application Areas

1. Medical Diagnosis

Combining test results with prior disease prevalence. "The test is 99% accurate, but the disease only affects 1 in 10,000 people. Probability you actually have it? Still very low."

2. Robotics & SLAM

Simultaneous Localization and Mapping. Updating the car's belief about where it is in 3D space as it sees new landmarks.

3. Quantitative Finance

Estimating the volatility of assets while incorporating "priors" about market crashes (Black Swan events).

2. ML Connection: Active Learning

Active Learning Cost Optimization

Sampling by "Surprise"

In many ML tasks, labeling data is expensive (e.g., asking a doctor to label X-rays).

A Bayesian model doesn't ask for labels at random. It calculates the Posterior Variance for every unlabeled image. - If the variance is Low, the model is confident. Don't waste money labeling it. - If the variance is High, the model is confused. **This is the image we ask the human to label.**

By using Bayesian Uncertainty to drive the labeling process, companies can train models that are 90% accurate using only 10% of the usual data labeling budget.
