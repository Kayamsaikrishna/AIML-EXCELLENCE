Mutual Information Definition

Measuring the "Bond" between variables. How much does knowing \(X\) tell us about \(Y\), and vice versa?

The Shared Secret

Mutual Information is the amount of information that is common to both variables. If Entropy is the uncertainty of one variable, and Joint Entropy is the uncertainty of both, Mutual Information is the "Overlap" area in the Venn diagram.

1. Definition

Mutual Information \(I(X; Y)\) of two random variables \(X\) and \(Y\) is defined as:

$$ I(X; Y) = \sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} \sum_{y \in \mathcal{Y}} p(x, y) \log_2 \frac{p(x, y)}{p(x)p(y)} $$

Interpretation via Distance: Mutual information measures how far the joint distribution \(p(x, y)\) is from being independent \(p(x)p(y)\).
- If \(I(X; Y) = 0\), the variables are Independent. - If \(I(X; Y)\) is high, the variables are Highly Related.

2. The Entropy Formulas

Mutual Information can be derived from existing entropy measures in four symmetric ways:

$$ I(X; Y) = H(X) - H(X|Y) $$ $$ I(X; Y) = H(Y) - H(Y|X) $$ $$ I(X; Y) = H(X) + H(Y) - H(X, Y) $$ $$ I(X; Y) = I(Y; X) \quad \text{(Symmetry)} $$

3. Relationship to KL Divergence

Mutual information is simply the Kullback-Leibler (KL) Divergence between the joint distribution and the product of the marginals:

$$ I(X; Y) = D_{KL}(P(X, Y) \| P(X)P(Y)) $$

This is a powerful realization because it means we can use tools designed for measuring probability distances to estimate variable dependencies.

Deep Learning Representation Learning

The Information Bottleneck

In deep neural networks, we have an Input (\(X\)), an Embedding or Hidden Layer (\(Z\)), and a Target (\(Y\)).

  • Objective: We want to maximize the information between the hidden layer and the target (\(I(Z; Y)\)).
  • Constraint: We want to minimize the information between the hidden layer and the raw input (\(I(Z; X)\)).

This "Bottleneck" forces the model to Discard irrelevant noise from the input while Retaining only the bits necessary for the task. This is the mathematical reason why deeper layers look like abstract concepts rather than pixels.
