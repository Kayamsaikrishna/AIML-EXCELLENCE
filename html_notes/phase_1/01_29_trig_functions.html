1. Definitions

For an angle \( \theta \) on the Unit Circle:

  • Sine: Vertical coordinate (Height). \( y = \sin(\theta) \)
  • Cosine: Horizontal coordinate (Width). \( x = \cos(\theta) \)
  • Tangent: Slope. \( \tan(\theta) = \frac{\sin(\theta)}{\cos(\theta)} \)

2. Processing Sound (FFT)

AI models like Whisper (ASR) don't process raw audio waves directly. They process Spectrograms.

A Spectrogram is created by running a Fast Fourier Transform (FFT), which decomposes complex sounds into simple Sine and Cosine waves of different frequencies.

$$ F(\omega) = \int f(t) e^{-i \omega t} dt $$

3. Generating Waves

Synthesizing a signal.

import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# 1 second of audio at 44.1kHz
sample_rate = 44100
t = np.linspace(0, 1, sample_rate)

# Create a chord (C Major: C4, E4, G4)
# Frequencies: 261.63, 329.63, 392.00 Hz
freqs = [261.63, 329.63, 392.00]

waveform = np.zeros_like(t)
for f in freqs:
    waveform += np.sin(2 * np.pi * f * t)

# Provide this array to a model, and it detects "C Major".
print(f"Generated {len(waveform)} samples of audio data.")
