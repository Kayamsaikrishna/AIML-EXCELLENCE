10. Triple Vector Product

The BAC-CAB Identity and Vector Simplification.

1. The BAC-CAB Identity

The Triple Vector Product \( \mathbf{u} \times (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w}) \) results in a vector. It is restricted to the plane formed by \( \mathbf{v} \) and \( \mathbf{w} \).

$$ \mathbf{u} \times (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w}) = \mathbf{v}(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{w}) - \mathbf{w}(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}) $$

Why simplify?

Cross products are computationally more expensive than dot products. Using the identity allows hardware accelerators to process complex geometric transformations using simpler arithmetic.

2. Complex Transformations in Robotics

Angular Velocity & Acceleration

In Reinforcement Learning for Robotics (like humanoid walking), calculating centripetal acceleration frequently involves triple vector products of the form \( \mathbf{\omega} \times (\mathbf{\omega} \times \mathbf{r}) \).

  • Simplification: Using the identity, this becomes \( \mathbf{\omega}(\mathbf{\omega} \cdot \mathbf{r}) - \mathbf{r}(\mathbf{\omega} \cdot \mathbf{\omega}) \).
  • Real-time Physics: This reduction is critical for high-frequency physics engines (1000Hz controllers).

3. Python: Verifying the Identity

import numpy as np

u = np.random.rand(3)
v = np.random.rand(3)
w = np.random.rand(3)

# Direct calculation
res1 = np.cross(u, np.cross(v, w))

# BAC-CAB calculation
res2 = v * np.dot(u, w) - w * np.dot(u, v)

# Verify they are identical
print(f"Verification: {np.allclose(res1, res2)}") # Output: True
