1. Definition

A Matrix is a rectangular array of numbers arranged in rows and columns.

$$ A = \begin{bmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12} & \dots & a_{1n} \\ a_{21} & a_{22} & \dots & a_{2n} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ a_{m1} & a_{m2} & \dots & a_{mn} \end{bmatrix} $$
  • Dimension: \( m \times n \) ($m$ rows, $n$ columns).
  • Element: \( a_{ij} \) is in row $i$, column $j$.
  • Mnemonics: RC Cola (Rows first, Columns second).

2. Tensors in Deep Learning

In AI, we generalize matrices to Tensors. A standard color image is a Rank-3 Tensor.

  • Height ($H$): Number of pixel rows.
  • Width ($W$): Number of pixel columns.
  • Channels ($C$): Color depth (e.g., 3 for RGB).

Shape: \( (H, W, C) \). A batch of images is Rank-4: \( (N, H, W, C) \).

3. Shaping Data

Manipulating shapes in NumPy.

import numpy as np

# Create a 2x3 Matrix
A = np.array([
    [1, 2, 3],
    [4, 5, 6]
])

print(f"Matrix A:\n{A}")
print(f"Shape: {A.shape}") # (2, 3)
print(f"Element a_21: {A[1, 0]}") # 4 (Zero-indexed)

# Reshaping (Critical for Neural Nets)
# Flattening layer
flat = A.reshape(-1) 
print(f"\nFlattened: {flat}")

# Adding a 'Batch' dimension
batched = A.reshape(1, 2, 3)
print(f"Batched Shape: {batched.shape}") # (1, 2, 3)
