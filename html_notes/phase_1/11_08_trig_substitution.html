Calculus 1.4 Geometry

Trigonometric Substitution

Turning square roots into circles.

The Hidden Circle

Algebra hates square roots like \(\sqrt{1-x^2}\). But Trigonometry loves them, because they represent the sides of a right triangle. We map the algebraic problem to a geometric one.

1. The Mapping

Expression Substitution Identity
\(\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}\) \(x = a \sin \theta\) \(1 - \sin^2 = \cos^2\)
\(\sqrt{a^2 + x^2}\) \(x = a \tan \theta\) \(1 + \tan^2 = \sec^2\)
\(\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}\) \(x = a \sec \theta\) \(\sec^2 - 1 = \tan^2\)

2. AI/ML Connection: High-Dimensional Spheres

The Curse of Dimensionality

AI models operate in high-dimensional spaces (d > 1000). To understand distances in these spaces, we need to integrate over the volume of a hypersphere.

The math of Trig Substitution generalizes to \(N\)-dimensions (using spherical coordinates). It leads to the counter-intuitive result that in high dimensions, most of the volume of a sphere is concentrated near the surface (the "shell").

This geometric fact explains why L2 Normalization (projecting vectors onto the unit sphere) is so effective in stabilizing Neural Networks (BatchNorm/LayerNorm).

3. Python: Solving the Circle

circle_area.py
import sympy as sp

x = sp.Symbol('x')
a = sp.Symbol('a', positive=True)

# Integral for Area of upper semi-circle: sqrt(a^2 - x^2)
f = sp.sqrt(a**2 - x**2)

# SymPy can solve this directly
result = sp.integrate(f, x)

print(f"Indefinite Integral: {result}")
# The result involves asin(x/a), confirming the trig sub relationship.

# Definite Integral for full circle area (2 * integral -a to a)
area = sp.integrate(f, (x, -a, a))
print(f"Area of Semi-Circle: {area}")
print(f"Full Circle Area: {2*area}") # pi * a^2
