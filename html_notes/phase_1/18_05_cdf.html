Prob 1.4 Accumulation

Cumulative Distribution Function (CDF)

How much probability is behind you?

Definition

The CDF \(F_X(x)\) gives the probability that \(X\) takes a value essentially less than or equal to \(x\). \[ F_X(x) = P(X \le x) \] It is the integral of the PDF: \[ F_X(x) = \int_{-\infty}^{x} f_X(t) dt \] It always starts at 0 and grows monotonically to 1.

2. ML Connection: KS Test

Comparing Distributions

How do we know if our data follows a specific distribution? The Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) Test compares the empirical CDF of the data against the theoretical CDF of the reference distribution. It basically measures the maximum vertical distance between the two CDF curves.
