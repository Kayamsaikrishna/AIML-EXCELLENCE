07. Normal-Normal Conjugate Pair

Updating a Gaussian belief with Gaussian evidence.

1. The Mathematics

In many real-world scenarios, we are estimating a continuous value (like the height of a person or the weight of a model parameter). If the Likelihood is Normal (Gaussian) and the Prior is also Normal, the Posterior is guaranteed to be Normal.

$$ \mu_{post} = \frac{\frac{\mu_{prior}}{\sigma_{prior}^2} + \frac{n\bar{x}}{\sigma_{data}^2}}{\frac{1}{\sigma_{prior}^2} + \frac{n}{\sigma_{data}^2}} $$

The posterior mean is a **Weighted Average** of the prior mean and the sample mean, weighted by their respective Precisions (1/Variance).

2. Shrinkage and Information

The Normal posterior reflects the "Shrinkage" effect: the data pulls our initial guess toward the truth. Because the math is just a simple weighted sum, it is extremely fast and requires no simulation.

3. AI/ML Connection: Kalman Filters and Meta-Learning

Real-time Parameter Tracking

In AI, the Normal-Normal pair is the engine of Kalman Filtering.

If you are building an Autonomous Drone, its position estimate is a Gaussian prior. The sensor data (GPS/IMU) provides a Gaussian likelihood. By using the Normal-Normal update, the drone updates its position in **Microseconds**. This is also how Meta-Learning (MAML) works at its core: the "Base Weights" act as a Gaussian prior. When a new task arrives with only 5 data points, the model performing a "Fast Update" is essentially doing a Bayesian Normal-Normal shift to adapt to the new task without forgetting the general rules of the world.

4. Python: Updating a Mean

# Prior: Mean 0, Variance 1
mu_p, var_p = 0.0, 1.0
# Data: 10 points with mean 2.5, known variance 0.5
n, x_bar, var_d = 10, 2.5, 0.5

# Precision-weighted update
precision_p = 1/var_p
precision_d = n/var_d

mu_post = (mu_p * precision_p + x_bar * precision_d) / (precision_p + precision_d)
var_post = 1 / (precision_p + precision_d)

print(f"Posterior: N({mu_post:.2f}, {var_post:.2f})")
# mu_post will be close to 2.5 because the data precision is high.
