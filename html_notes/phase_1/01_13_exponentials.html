1. The Definition of \( e \)

The only function where the rate of growth is equal to the current size.

$$ \frac{d}{dx} e^x = e^x $$

This property is famously why Backpropagation is so elegant. The local gradient of \( e^x \) is just its output value!

2. The Logits to Probs Pipeline

Raw Neural Network outputs are called Logits (can be \( - \infty \) to \( + \infty \)). We use exponentials to turn them into probabilities (0 to 1).

Softmax Formula

$$ \text{Softmax}(z_i) = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_{j} e^{z_j}} $$

Why Exponentials? Because they:

  1. Turn negatives into small positives (Probabilities must be positive).
  2. Amplify the largest value (Winner-take-all dynamics).

3. Softmax from Scratch

Implementing the core of Attention mechanisms.

import numpy as np

def softmax(logits):
    # Tip: Subtract max for numerical stability (prevent overflow)
    exp_vals = np.exp(logits - np.max(logits))
    return exp_vals / np.sum(exp_vals)

# Example: Classification Output (Logits)
logits = np.array([2.0, 1.0, 0.1])

probs = softmax(logits)
print(f"Logits: {logits}")
print(f"Probs: {probs}") 
# [0.659, 0.242, 0.099] -> Sums to 1.0

# Note how 2.0 isn't just "double" 1.0. 
# In exp space, it's roughly e^1 = 2.7x more probable!
