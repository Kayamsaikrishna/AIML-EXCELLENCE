Dealing with scale. How we handle numbers that grow too fast ($e^x$) or vary too widely ($\log x$).

1. Exponentials & Laws of Exponents

Repeated multiplication. The language of explosion.

Key Laws

  • \((a^m)(a^n) = a^{m+n}\) (Adding powers)
  • \((a^m)^n = a^{mn}\) (Multiplying powers)
  • \(a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}\) (Inversion)

AI Application: The Softmax Function

In Classification, we want probabilities. But neural network outputs (logits) can be negative ($-\infty$ to $+\infty$).

We use \(e^x\) to force everything to be Positive.

$$ \text{Softmax}(z_i) = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum e^{z_j}} $$
