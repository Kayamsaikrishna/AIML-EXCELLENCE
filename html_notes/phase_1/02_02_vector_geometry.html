02. Geometric Interpretation

Visualizing data as arrows in space.

1. Magnitude & Direction

A vector is not just a point; it is a translation. It describes how to get from the origin \((0,0)\) to a specific location in space.

$$ \|\mathbf{v}\| = \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2 + \dots + v_n^2} $$

2. Vectors in AI Architecture

Latent Space & Embeddings

In Generative AI (like Midjourney or ChatGPT), images and words are mapped to a "Latent Space". If two words have vectors pointing in the same direction, they likely share a semantic meaning.

  • Direction = Semantics: Vectors for "King" and "Queen" point in similar directions.
  • Magnitude = Intensity: The length of the vector can represent the confidence or strength of a feature.

3. Python: Calculating Magnitude

import numpy as np

v = np.array([3, 4])

# Calculating manually
magnitude_manual = np.sqrt(np.sum(v**2))

# Calculating using Numpy Linear Algebra module
magnitude_linalg = np.linalg.norm(v)

print(f"Magnitude: {magnitude_linalg}") # Output: 5.0
