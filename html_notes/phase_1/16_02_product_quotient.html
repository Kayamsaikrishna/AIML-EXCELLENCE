02. Product and Quotient Rules

Handling interacton between functions.

1. The Product Rule

The derivative of a product is NOT the product of the derivatives. Instead:

$$ \frac{d}{dx} [f(x)g(x)] = f'(x)g(x) + f(x)g'(x) $$

Mnemonic: *"First d-Second plus Second d-First"*.

2. The Quotient Rule

For a ratio of functions:

$$ \frac{d}{dx} \left[ \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} \right] = \frac{f'(x)g(x) - f(x)g'(x)}{[g(x)]^2} $$

Mnemonic: *"Low d-High minus High d-Low, square the bottom and away you go!"*

3. AI Connection: Softmax and Attention

The Softmax Gradient

The Softmax function \(\frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum e^{z_j}}\) is a quotient. Calculating its derivative requires the Quotient Rule (or a trick with the Product Rule). The fact that the derivative depends on all other outputs is why Softmax "couples" the neurons together during training, forcing them to compete for probability mass.

4. Python: Symbolic Verification

import sympy as sp

x = sp.Symbol('x')
u = x**2
v = sp.sin(x)

# Product Rule
prod_diff = sp.diff(u * v, x)
print(f"d/dx(x^2 * sin x) = {prod_diff}")

# Quotient Rule
quot_diff = sp.diff(u / v, x)
print(f"d/dx(x^2 / sin x) = {sp.simplify(quot_diff)}")
