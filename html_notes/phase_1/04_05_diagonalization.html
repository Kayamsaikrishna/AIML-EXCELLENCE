1. Definition

If \( A \) has \( n \) independent eigenvectors, we can put them into columns of a matrix \( P \) (Eigenvector Matrix) and the eigenvalues into a diagonal matrix \( D \).

$$ A = P D P^{-1} $$

This implies \( A^k = P D^k P^{-1} \), making powers of matrices instant to compute.

2. Spectral Domains

In Graph Neural Networks, "Graph Convolutions" are often defined in the spectral domain. This means we Diagonalize the Graph Laplacian \( L = U \Lambda U^T \).

Filtering simply becomes element-wise multiplication on the diagonal eigenvalues \( \Lambda \), which is computationally efficient.

3. Computing Powers

import numpy as np

A = np.array([
    [4, 2],
    [1, 3]
])

val, P = np.linalg.eig(A)
D = np.diag(val)
P_inv = np.linalg.inv(P)

print("A (Original):")
print(A)

print("\nReconstructed (PDP^-1):")
print( (P @ D @ P_inv).real.round(2) )

# Calculate A to the power of 10
# Naive: A @ A @ ... (10 times)
# Smart: P @ D^10 @ P^-1
D_10 = np.diag(val ** 10)
A_10 = P @ D_10 @ P_inv

print("\nA^10 (Computed via Eigen-decomp):")
print(A_10.real.round(0))
