Combinatorics Patterns

Binomial Coefficients & Pascal's Triangle

Complexity from Simplicity. Exploring the universal triangle where every number is the sum of its parents — a structure that appears in everything from probability to deep learning geometry.

The Recursive Nature of Choice

The number of ways to pick a team (\(\binom{n}{r}\)) can be calculated directly. But it also has a recursive secret: To pick \(r\) people from \(n\), you either pick the first person (then you need \(r-1\) more from the remaining \(n-1\)) OR you don't pick the first person (then you need to pick all \(r\) from the remaining \(n-1\)).

1. Pascal's Identity

$$ \binom{n}{r} = \binom{n-1}{r-1} + \binom{n-1}{r} $$

This simple addition is the engine that builds Pascal's Triangle.

2. Pascal's Triangle

A geometric arrangement of binomial coefficients. Each row \(n\) represents the coefficients of the expansion \((x+y)^n\).

            1
           1 1
          1 2 1
         1 3 3 1
        1 4 6 4 1

Row Sum

The sum of all entries in row \(n\) is \(2^n\). This is because the total number of subsets of a set of size \(n\) is \(2^n\).

Symmetry

The triangle is perfectly symmetrical because \(\binom{n}{r} = \binom{n}{n-r}\).

Probability Neural Nets

The Triangle in AI

  • Bernoulli Trials: The rows of Pascal's triangle tell us the "likelihood" of different outcomes in binary trials (like flipping coins). Row 4: 1, 4, 6, 4, 1. If you flip 4 coins, there are 6 ways to get exactly 2 heads.
  • CNN Receptive Fields: When you stack convolutional layers, the "weight" that different input pixels have on the final output often follows a distribution that resembles a row in Pascal's triangle (approaching a Gaussian curve).
  • Attention Span: In some specialized Attention mechanisms, weights are distributed using binomial coefficients to penalize tokens that are too far away from the center.
  • Overfitting: When a model memorizes "too many" patterns (\(2^n\) combinations), it is exploring the total sum of Pascal's row. Regularization helps keep the model focused on the "middle" coefficients where the most general features live.
