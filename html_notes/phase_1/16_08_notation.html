08. Leibniz, Lagrange, and Newton Notation

Decoding the symbols of the derivative.

1. Lagrange Notation (Prime)

$$ f'(x), \quad f''(x), \quad f^{(n)}(x) $$

Use: Compact, great for simple functions and Taylor series.

2. Leibniz Notation (Fraction)

$$ \frac{dy}{dx}, \quad \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}, \quad \frac{d}{dx}f(x) $$

Use: Explicitly shows the variables. The "gold standard" for the Chain Rule because terms look like they "cancel". Essential for AI research papers.

3. Newton Notation (Dot)

$$ \dot{y}, \quad \ddot{y} $$

Use: Specifically for Time derivatives (\( \frac{dy}{dt} \)). Dominant in Robotics and Control Systems.

4. AI Connection: Partial Derivative Symbols

In AI, we rarely have just one variable. We use the Jacobi/Partial symbol: $$ \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_i} $$ This tells us we are differentiating the Loss \(L\) with respect to weight \(w_i\) while holding all other millions of weights constant. It's the multi-dimensional evolution of Leibniz notation.
