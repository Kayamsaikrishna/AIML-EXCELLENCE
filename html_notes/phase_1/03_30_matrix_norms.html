1. Definitions

Frobenius Norm: Treat matrix like a long vector and take Euclidean norm.

$$ ||A||_F = \sqrt{\sum |a_{ij}|^2} $$

Operator (Spectral) Norm: The maximum factor the matrix can stretch a vector.

$$ ||A||_2 = \max_{x \neq 0} \frac{||Ax||}{||x||} = \sigma_{\max}(A) $$

2. GAN Stability

In Generative Adversarial Networks (WGAN-GP), we need the Discriminator to be 1-Lipschitz.

This means the gradient norm must be bounded. This is strictly related to the Spectral Norm of the weight matrices. Spectral Normalization enforces this by dividing Weights by their spectral norm \( W_{new} = W / \sigma(W) \).

3. Calculating Strech

Comparing Frobenius vs Spectral.

import numpy as np

A = np.array([
    [1, 100],
    [0, 1]
])

# Frobenius: sqrt(1+10000+0+1) ~ 100
norm_f = np.linalg.norm(A, 'fro')

# Spectral: Max Singular Value
# It will be slightly larger than 100
norm_2 = np.linalg.norm(A, 2)

print(f"Frobenius Norm: {norm_f:.2f}")
print(f"Spectral Norm:  {norm_2:.2f}")

# Spectral tells us the worst-case amplification
v = np.array([0, 1])
print(f"Stretch on [0, 1]: {np.linalg.norm(A @ v)}") 
# 100.04 -> Matches Spectral
