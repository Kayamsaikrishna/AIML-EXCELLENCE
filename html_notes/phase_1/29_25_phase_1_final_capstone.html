Phase 1: Final Capstone

From Bits to Brains: The Unified Theory of AI Foundations.

1. The Probability & Stats Pillar

You have completed 36 Series and hundreds of modules. You have moved from the basic laws of probability (Series 24) to the cutting edge of Information Theory (Series 36).

$$ \text{Data} \xrightarrow{\text{Stats}} \text{Information} \xrightarrow{\text{Calculus}} \text{Learning} $$

2. The Unified Map

  • Probabilistic Engine: Gaussian distributions, Bayes' Theorem, and Moments.
  • Inference Engine: p-values, Hypothesis Testing, and Regressions.
  • Optimization Engine: MSE, Cross-Entropy, and KL Divergence.
  • Causal Engine: Experimental Design, Directed Info, and A/B Testing.

3. Transitioning to Phase 2: Classical Machine Learning

Mathematics is the "Why" and the "How." In Phase 2, we begin the "What." We will use everything learned here to build:

  • Support Vector Machines (Linear Algebra)
  • Decision Trees (Information Theory)
  • Gradient Boosting (Calculus & Stats)
  • Principal Component Analysis (Linear Algebra)
  • Naive Bayes (Probability)
  • Hidden Markov Models (Probability)

Congratulations!

You now possess the mathematical intuition of a world-class AI researcher. You don't just "Run models"—you understand the Entropy, the Curvature, and the Probabilistic Logic behind every weight update.
