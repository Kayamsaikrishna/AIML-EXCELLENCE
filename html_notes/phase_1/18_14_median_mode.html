Prob 1.4 Robustness

Median & Mode

Alternatives to the Mean.

Definitions

Median: Middle value (50th percentile). Robust to outliers. Mode: Most frequent value (Peak of PDF/PMF).

2. ML Connection: Loss Functions

L1 vs L2 Loss

  • MSE (L2 Loss): Minimizing it estimates the Mean. Sensitive to outliers.
  • MAE (L1 Loss): Minimizing it estimates the Median. Robust to outliers.
If your data has crazy outliers, training with L1 loss (MAE) is safer.
