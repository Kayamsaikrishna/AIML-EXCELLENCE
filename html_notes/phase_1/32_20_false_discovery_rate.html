20. False Discovery Rate (FDR)

The Benjamini-Hochberg approach: Balancing power and precision.

1. The Bonferroni Limitation

Bonferroni is "Safe" but "Weak." If you test 10,000 things, the threshold becomes so tiny that you miss almost every real effect. In massive datasets, we often care more about the Percentage of False Predictions among our findings, rather than ensuring *zero* false positives. This is the False Discovery Rate (FDR)**.

2. The Benjamini-Hochberg Procedure

This method ranks all p-values from smallest to largest and applies a adaptive threshold. It asks: "Out of all the things I claim are 'Discovered,' what percentage am I willing to admit are likely flukes?". Usually, we set FDR to 0.05—meaning 5% of our discoveries might be false alarms, but we gain massive **Power** to find the other 95%.

3. AI/ML Connection: Feature Discovery and Precision-Recall Tradeoffs

Finding Needles in the Haystack

In AI, FDR is the standard for Feature Importance Ranking at Scale.

If you are building a **Genomic AI** or a **Financial Market Predictor**, you have 100,000 possible inputs. Bonferroni would leave you with 0 features. **False Discovery Rate (FDR)** allows you to select a subset of 500 vital features while acknowledging that ~25 of them might be noise. This is much better than having zero! In Anomaly Detection** (like identifying fraudulent bank accounts), you use FDR to tune the "Sensitivity" of your AI. It allows the business to say: "We want to catch as many hackers as possible, as long as no more than 1% of our 'Flagged' accounts are actually innocent users." FDR provides the mathematical knob to tune that threshold perfectly.

4. Python: FDR Correction

# 100 experiments, many with small p-values but also noise
p_vals = np.random.uniform(0, 1, 100) # Pure noise
p_vals[:10] = 0.0001 # 10 real effects

# Apply FDR (fdr_bh = Benjamini-Hochberg)
reject, p_corr, _, _ = multipletests(p_vals, alpha=0.05, method='fdr_bh')

print(f"Discoveries: {sum(reject)}")
# FDR will likely find the 10 real ones while allowing 
# a few 'False Discoveries' to maintain high power.
