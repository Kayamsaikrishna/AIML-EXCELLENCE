Prob 1.4 Selection

Combinations

Choosing without ordering.

n Choose k

When order doesn't matter (a pizza with pepperoni and cheese is the same as cheese and pepperoni), we use Combinations: \[ C(n, k) = \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!} \]

2. ML Connection: Feature Selection

Subset Selection

If you have 100 features and want to find the best subset of 5 features to train your model, you have \(\binom{100}{5}\) possibilities (about 75 million). Since we can't test them all, we use greedy algorithms like Forward Selection or **L1 Regularization (Lasso)** to approximate the best combination.
