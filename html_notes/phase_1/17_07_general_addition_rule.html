Prob 1.4 Overlap

General Addition Rule

Don't double count.

Inclusion-Exclusion

If \(A\) and \(B\) overlap (intersect), summing their probabilities counts the overlap twice. We must subtract the intersection: \[ P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B) \]

This extends to multi-label problems where an outcome can belong to multiple categories simultaneously.

2. ML Connection: Union Bound

Boole's Inequality

In Learning Theory, we often want to bound the probability that *any* hypothesis in our model space makes a large error. The Union Bound states: \[ P(\cup A_i) \le \sum P(A_i) \] This is useful because calculating overlaps is hard, but summing individual probabilities provides a safe "Worst Case" upper bound (VC Dimension theory).

3. Python: Venn Logic

inclusion_exclusion.py
# Cards: 52 deck
# A: Heart (13 cards)
# B: Face Card (12 cards: J, Q, K * 4 suits)
# Overlap: Heart Face Cards (3 cards: J, Q, K of Hearts)

p_heart = 13/52
p_face = 12/52
p_overlap = 3/52

# P(Heart OR Face)
p_union = p_heart + p_face - p_overlap
print(f"Prob: {p_union:.4f}") # 22/52
